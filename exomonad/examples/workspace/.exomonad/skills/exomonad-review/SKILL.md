---
name: exomonad-review
description: Review a hosted agent's exact candidate and route findings or acceptance evidence to its owner. Load for independent code review and bounded repair.
---

Review substantial implementation against its exact source revision and accepted
contract. Inspect the production consumer, ownership boundaries, failure paths,
and cleanup behavior. Check the cumulative change from its stated base; a tip
commit alone may omit earlier edits. Keep source identity, executed check counts,
and scope of evidence explicit. A selected test with zero matches did not run.

Report actionable findings with the source location, defect, consequence, and
needed repair. Keep an implementer attached to repairs on the same paths. Review
a revised candidate at its new exact revision. Independent review should use an
agent whose context and source access support the question being asked.

A successful local review does not prove combined acceptance. The integration
owner checks the resulting revision and its interactions with other accepted
changes. A delivered report is not evidence that the owner read it or incorporated
its correction; request acknowledgment when that distinction changes the next
step.
