has an authenticated decision. Private source selection now composes against the

changed stale comparisons or normalized returned tokens without changing all of
their consumers. This is not evidence that the live-compiler architecture needs
replacing. It needs current private state and selected native support to travel
intact across the already-existing owners.

## Why the next private-state repair differs

Immediate compiler mutation during synchronous host acceptance is not the right
shortcut. A Job can be accepted while its enclosing checked item is parked on
that effect. The item already has an exact predecessor/successor relationship.
Keep acceptance after the original cancellation fence, record its exact delta,
settle that authored program, and apply accepted rows before new authored readiness
or freeze. Internal carrier preparation retains its separate limited purpose.

The refinement was challenged against hidden input preparation, prototype reuse,
multi-item programs, declaration-only first items, cancellation, freeze and real
request activation. The plan includes those distinct cases. It does not introduce
a second World registry, universal async task framework or generic event store.

Likewise, preserve exact selected HOME row identity at the existing issuer rather
than repairing Arc identity at the completion return. Payload equality permits
storage sharing; it does not establish common issuance or current-source authority.
