## Selected-row contract

One persistent selected-row map owns each actual canonical/Original acquisition,
its checked relationship, publication provenance and native decision. Decisions
distinguish unissued, issued-empty and exact issued groups with actual or explicitly
missing acquisition evidence. Whole-row Receiving/Incoming selection carries all
of these together. A losing row may add custody, never merge permission into the
winner. Missing acquisition cannot be discharged by an availability lookup.

Publication changes provenance on the selected row. Source extension, World
fragments, completed outputs, interface extension, narrowing and continuation
carry the actual producer's rows. Delete normal-live role-plus-inventory
reconstruction. Recorded recovery authenticates separately and still crosses
the existing contextual finish gate. Remove generic live equality; use named
fact comparisons or actual-owner comparisons appropriate to each consumer.

Derived record, home-unit, availability and transport indexes may remain private.
They cannot issue selected authority. Maintain changed entries and persistent
sharing; do not rebuild all records or scan all custody at each close.
