## Evaluation outcome

Correctness/review dogfood: strong partial success. Independent contexts found
real bugs: ignored parent-directory durability errors, stale authority/sequence
reuse after uncertain publication, retained-directory adoption allowing deletion,
loss of recovery ownership at timeout/drop, and startup cancellation racing cleanup
accounting. Some findings yielded root-integrated fixes; others are reviewed service
work with final host composition still pending. Historical WorktreeUnauthorized
causation is not proven by passing recursive-bootstrap regression.

Orchestration/product completion: mixed. Useful independent branches completed
and were folded; the service branch became a long prerequisite chain. Root allowed
that chain to dominate without an early enough intermediate product checkpoint.
A queued instrumentation request behind the long service continuation delayed
run-map completeness. More than60 service progress updates generated excessive
root wake/bookkeeping traffic. These are real costs, not evidence of speedup.

Expressive composition: demonstrated qualitatively. Retained typed evidence and
repair loops worked; tuple/applicative forks, fmap projections and traverse cleanup
were useful ordinary Haskell. Root and service retired completed actors through
retained typed handles rather than a new cleanup registry. No measured token,
provider-cache or dollar advantage over another system has been established.
General tuple binding was independently verified; the reported specific failure
remains unclassified without its original receipt, so no speculative patch was made.

## Changes for the next wave

Already changed: executable requestOptions/progress docs; source ownership and
failure contracts; strict uncertainty behavior; concrete recursive-bootstrap and
lost-owner regression checks. None implies the live host loaded new code.

Still to implement/validate: reliable bidirectional steering; matched mounted
service canary; projection-first/decision-bearing-progress defaults; stronger
checkpoint-versus-final delivery contracts; earlier independent handling of
cross-owner instrumentation; retention discipline before capacity saturation.
The32-descendant ceiling was reached;11 completed root/service actors were then
confirmed stopped, not a claim of filesystem/process reclamation or measured
post-cleanup capacity. Keep repair expertise deliberately, not every idle actor.

Ideal-system reading: observed composition and review fluency justify continuing.
Removing steering friction, evidence-dump repetition and retention pressure should
let that fluency pay off more often. Those remain testable hypotheses, not permission
to credit anticipated gains as measured performance. Next comparison should track
accepted outcome, critical-path time, coordinator interventions, discovery/recovery
work, provider-normalized prefix/usage and retained-idle occupancy separately.
