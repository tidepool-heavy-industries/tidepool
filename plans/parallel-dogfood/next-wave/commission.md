## Reusable commissioning shape

For a newly authorized campaign, give the planner the selected main/package,
concrete product outcome, acceptance boundary, preserved inputs, and explicit
exclusions. Ask for an executable decomposition in which a Sol coordinator and
substantial Sol owners retain meaningful integration work. Leads may explain or
challenge their understanding once; ordinary implementation and integration then
remain with Sol. Use fresh Astra consultations only for bounded consequential
decisions or repairs.

Fork related implementation from completed shared reasoning and the current bound
checkout. Record shared-file ownership before parallel edits, check the final
integration seams, and use targeted tests that prove the selected mechanisms.
Haskell owns mechanical routing; model turns own engineering decisions. External
supervision owns harness and resource monitoring. A historical checkpoint, build,
or launch authorization does not authorize another campaign.
